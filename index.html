About Kyle

With more than two decades in mortgage lending, Kyle Gillespie has built his business around a simple principle: clients make better decisions when they have clear information, thoughtful guidance, and an experienced team working ahead of the details.

Since 2003, Kyle has closed more than $1.25 billion in loan volume and has been ranked among the top 1% of mortgage originators nationwide by Scotsman Guide. He is also a Chicago Agent Magazine Who's Who honoree and a multi-year recipient of OriginPoint's Chairman's Circle Award, recognizing mortgage professionals who exceed $100 million in annual loan volume.

Kyle leads an experienced team that provides hands-on support from the first conversation through closing. Known for making complex financing easier to understand, they help clients evaluate their options, anticipate potential challenges, and move forward with confidence. Real estate partners value the team's proactive communication and ability to keep transactions on track.

A proud Chicagoan, Kyle lives in Lakeview with his wife and two daughters. He and his wife are active supporters of Hamilton Elementary School, where they regularly volunteer. Outside of work, Kyle enjoys coaching his daughter's travel softball team and playing golf along the lakefront.

Specialties

  • Fast-close programs — fast approvals and lock-before-you-shop programs.
